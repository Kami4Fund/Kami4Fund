Kami4Fund

    Welcome to Kami4Fund!

    Your place to secure funding for humanitarian projects with evidence-based funding unlocks based on goal achievement documentation. Inspired by the community charity roadmap at https://www.omikami.com and CZ's famous "four" image. In this case, funding is unlocked in four stages. Let's dig into the plan. (BTW: CZ has nothing to do with this, it's just a cool meme!)

    The workflow:

    Applicant connects their wallet and completes submission form that details overview of their project and the four funding tiers: Seed, Develop, Build, Scale with estimated costs and anticipated dates of completion for each tier.

    Omikami tokens are placed into the escrow dApp. As evidence for each tier is received and reviewed, tokens are released to the applicant's wallet.
